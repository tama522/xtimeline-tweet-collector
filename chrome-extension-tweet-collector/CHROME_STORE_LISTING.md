# Timeline Vault — Chrome Web Store Listing

## English (default)

**Title:** Timeline Vault — Local Timeline Archiver

**Summary (132 chars max):**
Capture and archive timeline posts locally. Full-text search, incremental backup, scheduled export. Privacy-first.

**Description:**
Timeline Vault automatically captures posts from your social media timeline as you browse, storing everything locally in your browser — no cloud, no accounts, no data leaves your device.

**Features:**
• Automatic capture — posts are saved as you scroll your timeline
• Multi-account support — track which account you were viewing when each post was captured
• Full-text search — instantly search through all saved posts
• Bookmark flagging — posts from your bookmarks page are tagged for easy filtering
• Translation capture — auto-translated posts include the translated text
• Incremental backup — export only new posts since your last backup
• Scheduled backup — set a daily time for automatic export
• Local-first — all data stays in your browser's IndexedDB
• No external requests — reads only what your browser already receives

**Privacy:**
Timeline Vault makes zero network requests. All data is stored locally in your browser. No analytics, no tracking, no cloud sync.
